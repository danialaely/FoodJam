  1. PACKAGE OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Juicy Celebrations includes:

  - 9 ready-to-use VFX prefabs (Landscape & Portrait variants)
  - 6 custom shaders optimized for particle effects
  - 11 pre-configured materials
  - 4 hand-crafted textures
  - 1 star mesh model
  - 1 FX Tester tool for quick previewing
  - 1 demo scene

All effects are designed to work out of the box. Simply drag a prefab
into your scene or instantiate it via script.

================================================================================
  2. REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

  - Unity 6 (6000.0) or later
  - Universal Render Pipeline (URP)
  - TextMeshPro (for FX Tester UI only)
  - New Input System (for FX Tester only)

  Note: The particle shaders (ConfettiParticle, GodRays, StarBurst,
  SoftAdditiveParticle, TrailGlow) use Built-in CG shaders which are
  compatible with both Built-in and URP pipelines.

  The StylizedStarUnlit shader is URP-native with full SRP Batcher support.

================================================================================
  3. QUICK START
================================================================================

  Step 1: Import the package into your Unity project.

  Step 2: Navigate to F_Juicy_Celebrations/Prefabs/ in the Project window.

  Step 3: Drag any prefab into your scene or Instantiate it via code:

      GameObject fx = Instantiate(celebrationPrefab, spawnPosition, Quaternion.identity);
      Destroy(fx, 8f); // Auto-cleanup after 8 seconds

  Step 4: Press Play. The effect will play automatically.

  That's it! For customization, see Section 10.

  5. PREFABS
================================================================================

  All prefabs are pre-configured and ready to use. Simply drag them into
  your scene or instantiate them from code.

  LANDSCAPE vs PORTRAIT:
  Effects come in two variants optimized for different screen orientations.
  Use Landscape for PC/Console and Portrait for mobile games.

  --- FX_Golden ---
  A premium golden victory celebration. Features golden confetti bursting
  upward, warm god rays expanding from center, and golden star explosions
  with glowing trails. Ideal for: Level completion, boss defeats, rewards.

  --- FX_Party ---
  A vibrant, multi-colored celebration with rainbow confetti, colorful
  god rays, and bright star bursts. Uses a wide color palette for a
  festive feel. Ideal for: Achievements, milestones, party moments.

  --- FX_Star_Explosion ---
  Focused star burst effects with dramatic spike animations and glowing
  trails. Creates a firework-like burst pattern radiating from center.
  Ideal for: Power-ups, item pickups, combo rewards.

  --- FX_Fireworks ---
  A sequential fireworks display with multiple timed bursts, each
  with trailing sparks. Creates an extended celebration moment.
  Ideal for: End-of-game celebrations, high scores, special events.

  --- FX_Godray ---
  Standalone god rays effect. A single rotating light beam pattern
  perfect for background ambiance or highlighting UI elements.
  Ideal for: Behind reward popups, title screens, item showcases.

  6. SHADERS REFERENCE
================================================================================

  All shaders are found under the "FateloomFx/" category in the shader
  dropdown menu when creating or editing materials.

  --------------------------------------------------------------------------
  6.1  FateloomFx/ConfettiParticle
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Purpose:  Confetti and small particle pieces with a card-flip animation.
  Blending: Alpha Blended (SrcAlpha OneMinusSrcAlpha)

  Properties:
    Particle Texture    Sprite texture for confetti shape
    Tint Color          Color multiplier applied to each particle
    Brightness          Overall brightness multiplier (0-3, default: 1.2)
    Flip Speed          How fast confetti pieces flip/tumble (0-20, default: 8)
    Edge Softness       Softens particle edges to avoid hard cuts (0-1)

  Usage Tips:
    - Assign different confetti shapes via the Particle Texture slot.
    - The flip effect uses Custom Data from the particle system. Enable
      Custom Data in your Particle System and send a random value to
      TEXCOORD1.x for varied flip timing per particle.
    - Increase Flip Speed for more energetic celebrations.
    - Keep Edge Softness low (0.05-0.1) for crisp confetti.

  --------------------------------------------------------------------------
  6.2  FateloomFx/GodRays
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Purpose:  Procedural rotating light beams radiating from center.
  Blending: Additive (One One)

  Properties:
    Ray Texture         Optional base texture (usually left white)
    Ray Color           Color of the light rays (RGBA, alpha controls opacity)
    Core Glow Color     Color of the bright center glow
    Intensity           Overall brightness (0-5, default: 1.5)
    Fade Start          Distance from center where rays begin fading (0-1)
    Fade End            Distance from center where rays fully fade (0-1)
    Pulse Speed         Speed of the breathing/pulse animation (0-5)
    Pulse Amount        Intensity of the pulse effect (0-1)
    Rotation Speed      How fast rays rotate. Negative = reverse (-2 to 2)
    Ray Count           Number of light beams (2-32, default: 12)
    Ray Sharpness       Edge sharpness of rays. Higher = sharper (0.1-10)
    Noise Scale         Amount of noise-based variation on rays (0-10)
    Noise Speed         Speed of noise animation (0-2)
    Vignette Strength   Edge darkening intensity (0-3)

  Usage Tips:
    - Use this on a single large billboard particle facing the camera.
    - Set Particle System Max Particles to 1 for best results.
    - Adjust Ray Count and Ray Sharpness together to control the look.
    - Lower Rotation Speed (0.1-0.2) for subtle, elegant rotation.
    - The shader is fully procedural — no ray texture is required.

  --------------------------------------------------------------------------
  6.3  FateloomFx/StarBurst
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Purpose:  Star/firework explosions with animated spike patterns.
  Blending: Additive (One One)

  Properties:
    Star Texture        Optional star shape texture
    Tint Color          Outer color of the star burst
    Core Color          Color of the bright center (usually white)
    Brightness          Overall brightness (0-8, default: 3.0)
    Glow Size           Radius of the soft glow around center (0-2)
    Spike Count         Number of star spikes/rays (2-12, default: 4)
    Spike Length         Length of each spike (0-2)
    Spike Sharpness     How sharp/thin the spikes are (0.1-20)
    Flicker Speed       Speed of brightness flickering (0-20)
    Flicker Amount      Intensity of the flicker effect (0-1)
    Rotation Speed      Spike rotation speed. Negative = reverse (-10 to 10)
    Fade Out Power      Controls how quickly edges fade to transparent (0.1-5)

  Usage Tips:
    - Works great with the Particle System Trails module enabled.
    - Each particle gets a unique flicker pattern via Custom Data.
    - Combine with TrailGlow shader for complete firework trails.
    - Use 4-6 Spike Count for classic stars, 2 for lens flares.

  --------------------------------------------------------------------------
  6.4  FateloomFx/SoftAdditiveParticle
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Purpose:  General-purpose soft particle shader with depth-based fading.
  Blending: Premultiplied Alpha (One OneMinusSrcAlpha)

  Properties:
    Particle Texture    Sprite/texture for the particle
    Tint Color          Color tint
    Intensity           Brightness multiplier (0-5, default: 1.0)
    Soft Factor         Depth-based soft fade distance (0-3, default: 1.0)
    Use Vertex Color    Toggle whether to use particle system colors

  Usage Tips:
    - Soft Particles require the camera to render a Depth Texture.
      In URP, enable Depth Texture in your URP Asset settings.
    - Increase Soft Factor for smoother blending with scene geometry.
    - Ideal for background glow particles, dust, and ambient sparkles.

  --------------------------------------------------------------------------
  6.5  FateloomFx/TrailGlow
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Purpose:  Glowing trail effect for particle trails.
  Blending: Soft Additive (SrcAlpha One)

  Properties:
    Trail Texture       Gradient texture for the trail
    Trail Color         Color tint (RGBA)
    Intensity           Trail brightness (0-5, default: 2.0)
    Fade Along Trail    How quickly the trail fades from head to tail (0-3)
    Width Fade          How quickly edges of the trail width fade (0-5)

  Usage Tips:
    - Assign this material to the Trail Material slot in the Particle
      System Renderer module (not the main Material slot).
    - Higher Fade Along Trail = shorter visible trail.
    - Higher Width Fade = thinner trail edges.

  --------------------------------------------------------------------------
  6.6  FateloomFx/StylizedStarUnlit
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Purpose:  Stylized unlit shader for 3D star meshes with fresnel rim
            glow. URP-native, SRP Batcher compatible.
  Blending: Configurable (default: Opaque)

  Properties:

    [BASE]
    Use Texture         Toggle texture sampling on/off (texture is optional)
    Texture             Base texture (only sampled when Use Texture is on)
    Base Color          Main color of the mesh
    Brightness          Overall brightness multiplier (0-10)

    [FRESNEL RIM]
    Enable Fresnel      Toggle fresnel rim glow on/off
    Fresnel Color       Color of the glowing rim edges
    Fresnel Power       Controls rim thickness. Higher = thinner rim (0.1-10)
    Fresnel Intensity   Brightness of the rim glow (0-10)

    [INNER GLOW]
    Enable Inner Glow   Toggle center glow on/off
    Inner Glow Color    Color of the center-facing glow
    Inner Glow Power    Focus of the glow. Higher = more focused (0.1-10)
    Inner Glow Intensity  Brightness of the inner glow (0-5)

    [EMISSION]
    Emission Color      HDR emission color (supports bloom)
    Emission Intensity  Emission brightness (0-15)

    [PULSE ANIMATION]
    Enable Pulse        Toggle breathing animation on/off
    Pulse Speed         Breathing speed (0-10)
    Pulse Min           Minimum brightness during pulse (0-1)
    Pulse Max           Maximum brightness during pulse (1-3)

    [HEIGHT GRADIENT]
    Enable Gradient     Toggle height-based color gradient on/off
    Top Color           Color at the top of the mesh
    Bottom Color        Color at the bottom of the mesh
    Gradient Offset     Shifts the gradient center point (-2 to 2)
    Gradient Range      Controls gradient spread (0.01-5)

    [RENDERING]
    Cull Mode           Back / Front / Off
    ZWrite              On / Off
    Src Blend           Source blend factor (for transparency)
    Dst Blend           Destination blend factor

  Usage Tips:
    - This shader requires NO texture. Use Base Color + Fresnel for a
      clean, stylized look on the included Mesh_Star_1 model.
    - For transparent/additive rendering, set:
        Src Blend = SrcAlpha, Dst Blend = One, ZWrite = Off
    - Enable Pulse for a breathing glow animation on idle stars.
    - Emission Color works with URP Post-Processing Bloom.
    - Height Gradient is great for making the star tips a different
      color from the center.

  9. FX TESTER TOOL
================================================================================

  The FX Tester is a runtime tool for quickly previewing and comparing
  effects during development.

  SETUP:
    1. Open the demo scene: Demo/Scenes/Scene_Demo.unity
       Or create your own setup:
       a. Create an empty GameObject in your scene
       b. Add the FXTester component (namespace: Fateloom)
       c. Add entries to the Effects list (name + prefab)
       d. Optionally assign Spawn Locations (Transform array)
       e. Assign a TextMeshPro UI text to the FX Name Display field

  CONTROLS:
    Q           Select previous effect
    E           Select next effect
    Space       Spawn selected effect
    Left Mouse  Spawn selected effect
    X           Clear all spawned effects

  INSPECTOR SETTINGS:
    Effects             List of FX entries (name + prefab pairs)
    Spawn Locations     Array of Transform points where effects spawn
    Cycle Spawn Points  If enabled, cycles through spawn points in order
    Camera Spawn Dist   Fallback spawn distance in front of camera (5m default)
    Auto Destroy Time   Seconds before spawned effects are destroyed (0 = never)
    FX Name Display     TextMeshProUGUI reference to show current effect name

  DEPENDENCIES:
    - New Input System package (com.unity.inputsystem)
    - TextMeshPro package (com.unity.textmeshpro)

  10. CUSTOMIZATION GUIDE
================================================================================

  --- Changing Colors ---

  Method 1: Edit the Material
    Select any material in Materials/ folder and adjust colors directly
    in the Inspector. Changes are immediate and saved automatically.

  Method 2: Particle System Color Module
    All shaders support Particle System vertex colors. Use the Color
    over Lifetime or Start Color modules to override colors per-particle
    without creating new materials.

  --- Adjusting Effect Timing ---

  Select the prefab and expand the Particle System hierarchy. Each child
  system controls a different layer of the effect:

    - Adjust Start Delay for staggered timing between layers
    - Modify Duration and Start Lifetime for effect length
    - Edit Emission > Bursts for the number and timing of particle bursts

  --- Making Effects Bigger/Smaller ---

    1. Scale the parent GameObject (uniform scale recommended)
    2. Or adjust Start Size in each Particle System's Main module
    3. For God Rays, increase Start Size to cover more screen area

  --- Creating Custom Color Variants ---

    1. Duplicate an existing material (Ctrl+D)
    2. Rename it (e.g., "Mat_Confetti_Blue")
    3. Adjust colors to your preference
    4. Assign the new material to your Particle System Renderer

  --- Landscape to Portrait Conversion ---

    1. Duplicate a Landscape prefab
    2. Open the prefab
    3. On the Shape module of confetti systems, reduce X scale and
       increase Y scale
    4. Adjust emission burst counts if needed (fewer for portrait)
    5. Reposition spawn areas to fit portrait dimensions

  --- Integrating with UI ---

    For effects behind UI popups:
    1. Place the effect in World Space behind your Canvas
    2. Or use a secondary Camera rendering to a RenderTexture
    3. Display the RenderTexture on a Raw Image in your Canvas

  --- Using with Bloom Post-Processing ---

    Effects look significantly better with URP Bloom enabled:
    1. Add a Volume to your scene (Global Volume)
    2. Add Bloom override to the Volume Profile
    3. Set Threshold: 1.0, Intensity: 0.5-1.0
    4. Star Burst and God Ray emission values are tuned to respond
       to bloom naturally

  11. PERFORMANCE TIPS
================================================================================

  The effects are optimized but here are tips for different platforms:

  MOBILE (iOS/Android):
    - Use Portrait prefab variants (fewer particles)
    - Reduce Emission burst counts by 30-50%
    - Disable Trails on StarBurst particles
    - Lower Max Particles to 100 for confetti systems
    - Use smaller texture sizes (32x32 for confetti)

  PC / CONSOLE:
    - Default settings are tuned for these platforms
    - Feel free to increase particle counts for richer effects
    - Enable Bloom post-processing for enhanced visuals

  GENERAL:
    - All particle systems use Looping = Off by default
    - Effects self-terminate after their Duration
    - Always Destroy() instantiated effects after they finish
    - Use object pooling for frequently spawned effects
    - God Rays use only 1 particle — minimal performance cost

  Approximate Particle Counts per Prefab:
    FX_Golden         ~200 particles peak
    FX_Party          ~250 particles peak
    FX_Star_Explosion ~120 particles peak
    FX_Fireworks      ~180 particles peak
    FX_Godray         1 particle

================================================================================
  12. TROUBLESHOOTING & FAQ
================================================================================

  Q: Effects appear pink/magenta.
  A: Shaders may need to recompile. Right-click the Shaders folder and
     select Reimport. If using URP, ensure the StylizedStarUnlit shader
     is used for mesh materials (URP-compatible).

  Q: Soft particles don't blend with the scene.
  A: Enable Depth Texture in your URP Renderer Asset:
     URP Asset > General > Depth Texture = On

  Q: God Rays don't appear or are too dim.
  A: Check that the Particle System Start Size is large enough (10-15).
     Also verify the material Intensity is above 0. God Rays use
     additive blending and may be invisible against bright backgrounds.

  Q: Effects don't play on Instantiate.
  A: Ensure Play On Awake is enabled on the root Particle System of
     the prefab. It is enabled by default on all included prefabs.

  Q: FX Tester script has compile errors.
  A: Install required packages:
     - Window > Package Manager > Input System (install/update)
     - Window > Package Manager > TextMeshPro (install/update)
     FX Tester is a demo tool only; removing it does not affect the
     VFX prefabs.

  Q: Star mesh appears flat/unlit.
  A: Ensure the material uses "FateloomFx/StylizedStarUnlit" shader.
     Increase Brightness and Emission Intensity. Enable Fresnel for
     visible rim lighting.

  Q: SRP Batcher shows "not compatible" on particle shaders.
  A: The particle shaders (ConfettiParticle, GodRays, StarBurst, etc.)
     use Built-in CG code for maximum compatibility. SRP Batcher
     compatibility is not required for particle materials as they use
     dynamic batching. Only StylizedStarUnlit is SRP Batcher compatible.

  Q: Can I use these effects in Built-in Render Pipeline?
  A: Yes. All particle shaders use Built-in CG code and work in both
     Built-in and URP pipelines. Only StylizedStarUnlit requires URP.
